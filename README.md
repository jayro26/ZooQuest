# ZooQuest - An Interactive, Console-Based Zoo Exploration and Investigation Game

## 📙 Description

A Java-based application where the user can explore different zones in a zoo and interact with various animals. Each zone has a mystery game, and the system will give the user a clue that can help solve the mystery. After getting enough clues and solving the mystery (find the animals), the user can unlock and move to the next zone until they finish the whole game.

## 🔎 Problems being addressed

Many children cannot visit physical zoos due to geographic distance, high travel costs, or lack of local facilities. This can also affect the teachers because they cannot organize field trips and need accessible digital tools to teach biodiversity. But children have easy access to the internet and gadgets to communicate with one another. At the same time, they only spend hours on gadgets (mostly computers) consuming passive content (like short videos) and online games, which disconnects them from learning about nature and wildlife. This also affects their parents because they struggle to manage excessive, unhealthy screen time at home. This system will bridge the educational divide by granting every child, regardless of family income or location a free access to wildlife learning, while redirecting mindless screen time toward interactive mystery-solving that builds early environmental empathy and critical thinking skills.

## 🎯 Target Users

1. **Children** - They can use this system to explore virtual zoos and their biomes, complete the task (find the animal), and learn their habitat, diet, and behavior. <br>
2. **Teachers/Parents** - Use the System as an interactive learning activity to introduce children to animals and wildlife.

## 🔒 Known Limitations
